레이아웃 규칙

GameplayRules.swift · fixed order

Portrait shell402 × 874 frame. Content max-width 640, centered. Ready sheet content max-width 720.
Lane area70% of gameplay width, centered. 4–7 lanes. Lane fill alpha 0.18; alternating lane fill uses 0.18 / 0.108.
Hit linemax(18% × height, 72) in SpriteKit coordinates. Receptor mirrors TapNote: laneWidth × 0.62 × 22, radius 8, stroke 2.
GuttersLife gauge left, score right. 16px life gauge, 20 segments, gap 3. Gutter panel width clamp(gutter - 12, 104, 180).
HUD panelCombo panel 200 × 100, top-center. Score/life panels share laneLine outline and dark theme surface.
Note geometryTapNote rounded rect: laneWidth × 0.62, height 22, radius 8 in scene geometry. Drag band: laneWidth × 0.45 + 2px core + cap 22px high.

Difficulty order · every screen and generator must preserve this order
