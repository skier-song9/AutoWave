레이아웃 규칙

GameplayRules.swift · fixed order

Landscape shell874 × 402 reference frame. Landscape-first shell; compact fallback only stacks content below 820px.
Lane field4 lanes span nearly the full screen width at the bottom, leaving HUD gutters. Inner lane field converges at the top-center vanishing region.
Lane edgesOuter and alternating boundaries use pink #F048C6 and cyan #38BDF8, 2px neon edges, bottom bloom. Lane surfaces stay dark translucent purple/blue.
Receptor padsRounded perspective pads at the bottom hit line. Radius 24 in SwiftUI geometry, 2–3px glowing gradient border, dark fill, faint ◇ or ✛ glyph.
HUD guttersLeft RingGauge owns HEALTH and 85% ring. Right ScoreRing owns SCORE 0012840 and COMBO 42. Circular gauges replace life bars and score panels.
Note geometryTapNote width laneWidth × .62, glossy capsule, white center dash slot. GemNote is a beveled hex plate. DragRibbon follows lane path and greys after break.
Modal surfaceReadyModal radius 28, near-black elevated fill, 1.5px purple→blue border, inner panels radius 16. CTA spans the right column bottom.
